## Resource estimate (per eml_gate, iCE40)

- 2 ROMs x 257 x W bits. At W=16/18 each fits in one 4Kbit EBR (256x16) plus a couple of fabric LUTs for the endpoint entry; **~2 EBR per gate** (of 30 on an iCE40 UP5K / 32 on HX8K).
- Interpolation: one (W+1)x4 multiply per LUT -> small fabric or shared SB_MAC16 slice; leading-one detect ~W LUT4s; k*ln2 one small multiply.
- exp_d2 after folding is **1 gate** (~2 EBR); ln_d4 is **3 gates** (~6 EBR) -- both comfortably fit even the UP5K.
- PoC RTL is combinational; production version registers the EBR reads and pipelines one stage per gate level (numerics unchanged).

## Caveats

- Q8.8 saturates the ln_d4 intermediate exp output (e^e/x reaches 151.5 at x=0.1, > 127.996); the table shows the resulting edge error. Q10.8 removes the overflow; Q10.12 shows the precision-limited floor.
- Synthesis (yosys/nextpnr) not run -- toolchain not assumed present; estimation-first per ROADMAP Track C staging.
